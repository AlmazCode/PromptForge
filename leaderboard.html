How places are calculated

Your points are the sum of your best saved score in each exercise: up to 160 across 16 exercises. Equal totals share a place.

Repeating an exercise adds points only when your best score improves. Pro changes the check limit, not the scoring rules.

Participation is optional. Only your display name, level and aggregate results are public. Leave in profile settings at any time.
