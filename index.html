Quarterly sales summary

Source data: Q3 revenue by month.

July
$142,000
August
$158,000
September
$135,000

First draft

Summarize sales.

The role, output format and scope are unspecified.

Revised prompt

You are a data analyst. Use the supplied Q3 sales data.

Write up to five numbered points in at most 150 words. Include total revenue, the strongest month and the August-to-September change.

Use a concise professional tone. Do not invent causes.

Example: 1. Total Q3 revenue was $435,000.

The revision specifies a role, output format, limits and an example.

Example revision, not an AI evaluation.
