Scoring criteria

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 10, then multiplied by its weight.

AI role

20%

Does the prompt assign a clear role to the AI?

Response format

25%

Is the output format clearly defined?

Response length

15%

Does the prompt limit the response length?

Response examples

20%

Does the prompt include at least one example?

Tone and style

10%

Does the prompt specify the tone and style?

Instruction clarity

10%

Is the task unambiguous and well-structured?

The weights sum to 100%. Your final score is out of 10.

Frequently asked questions

When is an exercise mastered?

The three courses group 16 exercises in a suggested order. An exercise is mastered when its best saved score is at least 8/10. A later lower score does not change this status. Deleting the best attempt may change it.

AI scores are approximate. Level points include the best result for every checked exercise, including scores below 8/10.

How can I compare attempts?

After two checks of the same exercise, you can compare scores and prompt text. Select two saved attempts to see the differences. Comparison does not use your check allowance.

Can I improve a prompt more than once?

Yes. Revise your prompt and compare the feedback for each attempt.

How are progress points calculated?

Keep the best score for each exercise and add those scores. A later, lower-scoring attempt does not remove points.

When do my free checks reset?

Your 24-hour period starts with your first successful check. Failed checks do not use your allowance.

What does test Pro include?

Unlimited daily checks for 30 days. No payment, no automatic renewal.

Glossary
Zero-shot
An instruction without examples of the desired response.
Few-shot
An instruction that includes examples to demonstrate the desired format or style.
System prompt
An instruction that sets the role, tone, and constraints for an AI conversation.
Temperature
A model setting that changes output variability. It is separate from the six criteria used to score a prompt.

Community guidelines

Privacy and form data

Leaderboard participation is optional. Joining publishes your display name, level and aggregate results, never your email, prompts or feedback. You can leave in profile settings without losing your private progress.

Practice streaks and weekly goals use calendar days in your device’s time zone. The weekly goal starts on Monday. Repeating an already mastered exercise does not count as new mastery.

PromptForge stores your profile, saved attempts and feedback messages on its server. Passwords are stored in a protected form. Drafts are saved in your browser and synced to your account when you are logged in. Clearing a form does not delete saved records.

When you check a prompt, its text and the exercise brief are sent to OpenAI for evaluation. Do not include personal or confidential information. Feedback messages are saved on the PromptForge server; they do not send an email or request a callback.
