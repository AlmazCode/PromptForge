01 / PROCESS

Evaluation process

AFTER SUBMISSION

A complete attempt has a prompt, six criterion scores, a weighted total, and concrete revision advice.

01
Draft Read the task and enter a prompt in the workspace.
02
Review Check role, format, length, examples, tone, and clarity.
03
Score Rate each criterion from 0 to 10 against the task requirements.
04
Weight Multiply each criterion score by its fixed rubric weight.
05
Total Add the weighted scores. The weights sum to 1.00, so the final score is out of 10.
06
Revise Read the criterion feedback, improve your prompt, and compare your next attempt.
02 / RUBRIC

Scoring criteria

WEIGHTED

Every exercise uses six criteria, each scored from 0 to 10. The weighted average produces the final score out of 10.

Evaluation rubric showing each criterion, what it checks, and example values.
Criterion What it checks Weight Example (good)
Role defined Is there an explicit role for the model? 20% You are a data analyst
Output format Is the response format specified? 25% Format: numbered list
Constraints Are length or content limits set? 15% no more than 150 words
Examples Are few-shot examples provided? 20% Example: 1. Revenue grew 12%
Tone Is the communication style defined? 10% Tone: professional, no filler
Task clarity Is the main task unambiguous? 10% Write a brief summary

Use the same rubric when drafting a prompt, reading its result, and comparing attempts.

03 / TERMS

Glossary

CORE CONCEPTS
Zero-shot
An instruction without examples of the desired response.
Few-shot
An instruction that includes examples to demonstrate the desired format or style.
CoT
A step-by-step approach to a task. In these exercises, focus on clear instructions and verifiable results.
Temperature
A model setting that changes output variability. It is separate from the six criteria used to score a prompt.
System prompt
An instruction that sets the role, tone, and constraints for an AI conversation.
04 / EXAMPLE

Prompt example

WORKED EXAMPLE

A quality prompt for “Sales data summarization”:

prompt.txt
6 CRITERIA 
                  You are a data analyst. Summarize the Q3 sales table.
Format: a numbered list of up to five items.
Tone: professional and concise.
Length: no more than 150 words.
Mention the total revenue, the strongest month,
and the change from August to September.
Example item: Q3 revenue totals $435,000.

This example includes a role, a clear task, an output format, a constraint, a tone, and examples — covering all six criteria in the rubric.

Worked calculation using illustrative criterion scores, not an AI verdict.

Final 1.00 8.60 / 10
05 / FAQ

Frequently asked

COMMON QUESTIONS
Can I improve a prompt more than once?

Yes. Draft a revision against the same rubric and compare the criterion feedback for each attempt.

Do I need a particular AI model?

No. The exercises teach instruction structure and can be drafted without calling an AI service.

How are progress points calculated?

Keep the best score for each exercise and add those scores. A later, lower-scoring attempt does not remove points.
