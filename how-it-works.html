How PromptForge works

Write instructions, check the six cues and revise. This website does not call an AI service.

Scoring criteria

  • AI role: 20%
  • Response format: 25%
  • Response length: 15%
  • Response examples: 20%
  • Tone and style: 10%
  • Instruction clarity: 10%

The demo looks for instruction keywords. It does not verify facts or generate an answer.

Frequently asked questions

What does the score mean?

The score only reflects six instruction cues. It does not prove that a prompt is correct.

Where are attempts saved?

Open Saved attempts to review or delete results. Data stays in this browser.

How is the total calculated?

The personal results table adds your best score for each exercise.

Does this site contact AI?

No. The keyword check runs in your browser and does not generate an answer.

Do I need a profile?

No. You can practise as a guest. A demo profile only adds a local name and email.

Feedback demonstration

Validate a sample message. Nothing is sent to a server.

Practice rules

Write respectful prompts. Check facts yourself and avoid sensitive personal data.

Browser data

Your demo name, email, drafts and attempts stay in this browser. Passwords and feedback messages are not stored. Clear site data to remove local records.
