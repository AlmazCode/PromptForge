Sales Data Summarization

Write a prompt that instructs an AI to summarize quarterly sales data into a concise numbered list.

6 criteria
2000 character limit
0 your attempts

Task

You are given a quarterly sales dataset for a SaaS company. Your goal is to write a prompt that will make an AI produce a brief summary of the key findings.

Requirements

  • Define the AI's role (e.g., data analyst)
  • Specify the output format — numbered list, max 5 items
  • Set a length constraint — no more than 150 words
  • Include a tone — professional, concise
  • Provide at least 1 example of desired output

Q3 orders: 5,660
August has the highest revenue and order count.
